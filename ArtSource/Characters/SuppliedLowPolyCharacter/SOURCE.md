# Supplied Low-Poly Character (Archived Reference)

This replacement-mesh experiment is not used by the active player. Visual
review found that its shoulder and hand topology did not deform cleanly with
the established combat rig. It is retained as a silhouette reference for the
geometry-only refinement of the proven seamless mannequin.

Original user-provided archive:

`ArtSource/Characters/SuppliedLowPolyCharacter/Original/low-poly-character.zip`

Original editable scene:

`ArtSource/Characters/SuppliedLowPolyCharacter/Original/MeinCharacter.blend`

The archive contained one 1,550-triangle character mesh with a single material.
It did not contain an armature, skin weights, animations, documentation, or a
license file.

The inactive gameplay-rig experiment is generated by:

`Tools/Blender/build_supplied_low_poly_character.py`

Generated editable scene:

`ArtSource/Characters/SuppliedLowPolyCharacter/SuppliedLowPolyCharacter_GameplayRig_v01.blend`

The derivative preserves the supplied topology, rotates its opposite
source-forward axis onto gameplay forward before fitting its A-pose to the
exact gameplay T-pose, and transfers four-influence weights to the unchanged
53-bone runtime skeleton. Performing the limb fit in the final coordinate frame
keeps shoulder and hand depth aligned with the gameplay bones. All transferred
thumb and finger influence is deliberately consolidated into the matching hand
bone, leaving the supplied hand surface rigid in its authored shape while the
finger-bone hierarchy remains available for a later hand replacement. The
outer shoulder cap uses a height-aware torso-to-upper-arm transition instead of
clavicle ownership, preventing its T-pose triangles from remaining as sleeve
flaps when the arms lower.
